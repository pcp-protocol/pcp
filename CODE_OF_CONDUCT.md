# Code of Conduct

PCP is built by people from many places and companies. We want taking part to be a good experience for everyone.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Argue about ideas, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and questions.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.
- Accept that a decision went against you, and say so if you think the process was unfair.

## What is not OK

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, or slurs and jokes about who someone is.
- Sharing someone's private information without their consent.
- Repeated off-topic disruption, or pushing a product in spec discussions.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any event or channel run for PCP. It also applies when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers at [pcp-conduct@phantazein.com](mailto:pcp-conduct@phantazein.com). Reports are kept confidential. If a maintainer is involved, they take no part in handling the report.

## What happens next

Maintainers review each report and respond fairly and in proportion: a private word, a warning, removal of content, or a temporary or permanent ban. The person reported can give their side before a ban.
